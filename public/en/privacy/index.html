NU-KYA! / PRIVACY

Information used by the game

This page describes information used by the current Nu-Kya! web features and the controls available to you. Contact fortelior@tendom.co.kr for privacy questions.

Game requests

The web game does not have a registration form. Your chosen teams, mode, staff setting, player searches and guessed player identifiers are sent to the game API. A server-issued game identifier appears in the game URL and is included when you share that URL.

Language preference

The browser stores your chosen language locally under nukya-language. It contains a language code used on future visits. You can remove it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Analytics

The site includes a Google Analytics integration. When enabled, Google may use cookies and similar technologies to process page usage and device or browser information. See Google’s partner-site data information. Browser cookie settings and the Analytics opt-out tool provide controls.

Ads and external services

When Google ads are enabled in the game, Google advertising scripts may load. Advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on earlier visits. Manage Google ad personalization in My Ad Center. Promotional links may include referral and selected-team parameters. Linked websites apply their own privacy practices. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Email and privacy requests

Sending email discloses your sender address, message and attachments to the recipient. Send only what is needed. Use the same address to request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided. Contact the service team for operational details about retention and deletion of server logs, game records, analytics and email.

Information date: September 17, 2026. This information is updated when features or information use change.
