About Nu-Kya!

Nu-Kya! is a free Korean baseball guessing game. It is about combining clues as well as remembering names: each guess tells you something about a hidden player.

The game

Find a player within nine guesses by comparing team, position, jersey number, throwing hand, batting side, birth year, height and weight. Choose a familiar team or several teams, and expand the pool to Futures players or staff. Korean and English interfaces are available.

Reference data

Use the roster date shown in the game as your reference. Transfers, registrations and profile corrections may not be reflected immediately. Check the official KBO website when you need the latest official information. A game result is not an official player record.

Advertising and links

Promotional banners may link to external services and are identified as advertisements. Google ads, when enabled, are not requested on setup, error or information pages. External services have their own terms and privacy practices.
